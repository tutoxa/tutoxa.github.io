Making Technical Careers Visible / SCHAU REIN!

An Instagram collaboration with Saxony’s SCHAU REIN! initiative to introduce technical apprenticeships and CLIMATECH.

Recruitment Marketing · Campaign Coordination

The context

The goal of the collaboration with SCHAU REIN!, Saxony’s career discovery initiative, was to introduce technical apprenticeships and CLIMATECH to people exploring vocational training.

My contribution
  • Handled external communication and coordination with SCHAU REIN!
  • Found a suitable participant for filming
  • Agreed the video concept and style
  • Filmed on two days at CLIMATECH
  • Edited and published collaborative Instagram content
The approach

I coordinated the production with the initiative and the filming participant. The agreed concept and style guided the two filming days at CLIMATECH and the edit for Instagram.

Deliverables & scope

The finished content was published jointly on Instagram and introduced technical apprenticeship opportunities at CLIMATECH.
