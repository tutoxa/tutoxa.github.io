CLIMATECH Montage GmbH · 2026

Making Technical Careers Visible / SCHAU REIN!

Making technical apprenticeships visible through a school visit, hands-on encounters and a published Instagram collaboration for CLIMATECH.

My roleConcept support, coordination with the initiative, on-site filming, editing and visual communication.

01 / Visit day02 / Instagram collaboration03 / Media coverage

01 / Visit day

A working day, up close.

The goal was to introduce pupils to technical apprenticeships and CLIMATECH through SCHAU REIN!. During the company open day, they explored the workshop, met employees and saw how technical tasks are approached in everyday work. I documented these encounters for the project’s visual communication.

02 / Instagram collaboration

From filming to a joint post.

I coordinated with the SCHAU REIN! initiative, found a suitable participant and helped agree the concept and visual style. Across two filming days at the company, I filmed and edited the content myself, shaping practical technical work into a short-form piece for Instagram. The collaboration content was published as a joint post as part of SCHAU REIN!.

I filmed and edited this collaboration content myself and prepared it for joint publication. The original Instagram post is linked here; its availability in the embedded player depends on Instagram.

03 / Media coverage

Visibility beyond the company.

A television crew accompanied the visit and recorded demonstrations and interviews. MDR later broadcast a report. My work was the event documentation and social media production; the television report was produced by the broadcaster.

The short clip shows the TV crew filming on site, not the finished report. Broadcast screenshot: MDR / supplied reference. A verified report link is still needed.
