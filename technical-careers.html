02 / CLIMATECH Montage GmbH / 2025–2026

Making Technical Careers Visible

Recruitment communication that connects career opportunities with the people doing the work.

Recruitment Marketing · Campaign Coordination

The context

I contributed to recruitment and apprenticeship communication, combining content production with coordination between employees, internal stakeholders and an external creative agency.

My contribution
  • Developed recruitment content ideas and formats
  • Coordinated with an external creative agency
  • Produced videos and Reels with employees
  • Supported Instagram, Facebook and Meta Ads activities
The approach

For SCHAU REIN!, Saxony’s career orientation initiative, I developed employee- and apprentice-focused content. One collaboration comprised two Reels and one social media post, covering format development, coordination, filming and editing.

Deliverables & scope

The work produced recruitment-focused social content and supported communication about technical careers. CLIMATECH also participated in a career orientation event covered by MDR television; I did not produce the MDR broadcast.
