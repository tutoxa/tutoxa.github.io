  1. 01 / 05

    The Opportunity

    A CLIMATECH technician was already sharing his everyday work with an established Instagram audience. I independently proposed an Instagram collaboration to bring his perspective into CLIMATECH’s employer branding.

    Follower counts are snapshots from the supplied profiles, not campaign results or evidence of follower growth. Screenshot dates were not supplied.

    Concept & Planning

    I developed the collaboration idea myself, selected an active project site and coordinated the shoot. For the interview, I planned the concept, script structure and questions.

06 / What I took from it

Takeaway

An employee’s existing voice gave the collaboration a clear point of view. Planning the interview alongside the rooftop shoot made it possible to turn one production into two Reels, an interview and a carousel.
