The Klimatechniker Project

An Instagram collaboration showing real technical work — from the initial idea to filming, editing and publication.

Project timeline

  3. 03Planning & Production

  1. 01 / 05

    The brief

    CLIMATECH wanted to draw more attention to its Instagram account and give potential applicants an accessible look at technical work. We used a real job site to show what the work involves and who does it.

  2. 02 / 05

    The idea

    A CLIMATECH technician was already sharing his everyday work with an established Instagram audience. I independently proposed an Instagram collaboration to bring his perspective into CLIMATECH’s employer branding.

    Follower counts are snapshots from the supplied profiles, not campaign results or evidence of follower growth. Screenshot dates were not supplied.

  3. 03 / 05

    Planning & Production

    I chose an active job site in Berlin and coordinated the shoot. I developed the interview concept, outlined the script and prepared the questions. On site, I filmed the installation work, everyday moments and the interview. I then edited short-form material for the collaboration and employer branding.

    More than 10 short-form video assets were produced during the project, including interview content, rooftop documentation, Reel-style edits and supporting employer-branding material.

    Bonus moment: trainee testimonial

    During the shoot, a trainee happened to be on site. I used the opportunity to capture a short testimonial, turning an unplanned moment into an additional employer-branding asset.

  4. 04 / 05

    Publication

    The content was shared through an Instagram collaboration with the employee’s account and CLIMATECH’s own channels, connecting an existing technical community with the company’s employer branding communication. I also helped prepare the finished content for publication.
